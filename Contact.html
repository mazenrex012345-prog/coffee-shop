Frequently Asked Questions

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery takes 1–2 hours within Kigali. Express delivery takes 30–45 minutes for an extra 1,000 RWF.

Is delivery free?

Standard delivery is free for orders above 10,000 RWF; otherwise it costs 1,000 RWF.

How can I pay?

Mobile Money, Visa/Mastercard, Rwandan debit card, bank transfer or Cash on Delivery. Payment on this site is a demonstration - no real money is charged.

Do I need an account to order?

Yes. Create a free account so you can place orders and follow their status under "My Orders".

Can I place a large catering order?

Of course - send us a message using the form above, or call +250 794 388 164.
