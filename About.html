About us

"Every cup tells a story, and every bite brings a smile."

Welcome to Ikawa House! Born in the heart of Kigali, we started with a simple passion: to brew the perfect cup of local Rwandan coffee and pair it with fresh, warm, and delicious snacks. We believe that your busy day shouldn't stop you from enjoying premium café quality, which is why we bring the coffee house experience directly to your home or office in Kicukiro and beyond!

What Makes Us Special?

  • Locally Sourced: We proudly use 100% premium Rwandan coffee beans, freshly roasted and brewed by expert baristas.
  • Baked with Love: Our snacks, croissants, and club sandwiches are prepared fresh every single morning.
  • Fast & Reliable: Hot and fresh delivery to your doorstep within 30-45 minutes.
  • Perfect For Every Moment: Whether it's your morning energy boost, a business meeting, or a special celebration, we've got you covered!
